Manage servers and Kubernetes clusters from Android with BoostSSH. Open SSH and Mosh sessions, troubleshoot workloads, organise connections and identities, and keep an encrypted SSH connection backup in your own Google Drive.

REMOTE ACCESS
• SSH terminal sessions with password or private-key identities
• Mosh, Telnet and a local shell
• Port forwarding, connection groups and reusable snippets
• Host-key verification for SSH and Mosh
• Terminal appearance and input settings

KUBERNETES WORKSPACE
• Import kubeconfig files, select contexts and switch namespaces
• Browse resources and CRDs, inspect health, conditions and events
• Follow container logs, open pod exec/attach terminals and transfer files
• Forward Pod and Service ports; connect directly or through a saved SSH route
• Edit and validate YAML, preview server dry-run results and apply changes
• Scale and restart workloads, manage rollouts and perform node maintenance
• Manage Helm releases and supported Argo CD and Flux resources
• View Metrics Server usage and optional Prometheus metrics when available

Cluster actions follow your Kubernetes permissions and the APIs/controllers installed in your cluster. Kubernetes profiles, credentials and drafts are encrypted locally. Kubernetes data stays on the device and is excluded from Drive sync, shared collections and SSH backup files.

ENCRYPTED BACKUP AND SYNC
Optionally connect your Google account to back up and sync personal SSH connections through Google Drive’s private application storage. Backup contents are encrypted on your device before upload. Save your recovery key so you can unlock your backup on another device. You can also import and export passphrase-encrypted local SSH backup files without connecting Google Drive.

SHARED COLLECTIONS
Share selected SSH connections with a team using Google Drive permissions and a separate collection code. Reader and editor roles control collection access. Personal connections stay separate, and you can keep a private identity on your own devices. Shared collections request additional Drive access.

YOUR DATA
BoostSSH has no developer account, advertising or developer analytics. Connections go directly to servers you choose. Optional backup and sharing use your Google Drive account. The developer cannot decrypt your Drive backup or recover a lost recovery key. See the privacy policy for details about Google services and user-requested connections.

Requires Android 16 or later. A Google account is optional for local terminal, Kubernetes and connection-management features. Remote connections require access to the destination server or cluster. Telnet does not provide encrypted transport.
