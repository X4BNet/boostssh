A TOOLBOX FOR YOUR CONNECTIONS

01

Connect and work

SSH and Mosh sessions, port forwarding, reusable identities, connection groups, and terminal settings. Telnet and a local shell are also available.

02

Keep an encrypted backup

Optionally back up and sync through your own Google Drive account. Personal backups use Drive’s private application storage and are encrypted on your device before upload.

03

Share a collection

Give a team access to selected connections using Google Drive permissions and a separately shared collection code. Keep personal connections separate.

YOUR DATA

No developer account.
No developer analytics.

Your saved connections and credentials are managed on your device. The developer does not receive your connection database, terminal sessions, or backup decryption keys. Google Drive is optional; local encrypted backup files work without it.

Google and the servers you choose have their own data practices. Read how backups, sharing and recovery work in our privacy policy.
